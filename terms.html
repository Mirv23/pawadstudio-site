Terms and privacy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Who we are

Paw Ad Studio is operated by Mirv Paul Létang, Port-au-Prince, Haiti, who is the party you contract with. You can reach us at hello@mirvpaulletang.me.

2. The service

We create advertising videos, images and copy for brands. We don't buy media, manage your ad account or launch your ads. The written proposal for your project lists what you will receive.

3. What we need from you

  • Real product photos or videos, your logo and your brand guide.
  • Proof for any claim you want in an ad.
  • Confirmation that you have the rights to everything you send us.
  • A clear approval or revision request within the time agreed.

4. Payment

Prices are in US dollars. For a pilot, you pay 50% to start and 50% before receiving the final files. We send a secure payment link by email. We may first share a watermarked preview. Monthly work is billed in advance each month.

5. Delivery and refunds

The 7-day delivery time starts when we have your deposit and complete brief. If we miss the deadline, you can ask for your deposit back. Delays caused by missing materials or late feedback extend the deadline. If we can't take your product, we refund your deposit in full before any work starts. Once we've started work, the deposit is otherwise non-refundable.

6. Revisions

A pilot includes 1 round of revisions. Monthly work includes 1 round per batch. A revision improves agreed work. A new product, angle or format is new work.

7. Using the finished ads

Once you've paid in full, you may use the delivered ads commercially for your brand. We may ask for permission before showing them in our portfolio.

8. AI tools

We use third-party AI tools for scripts, images, video and editing support. A person reviews the work before delivery.

  • These tools can produce results similar to what other users receive, so we can't guarantee that an ad is unique.
  • Materials we upload may be processed by these providers, and some providers may use them to improve their models. Tell us before sharing anything confidential, such as an unreleased product. We won't upload it without your written approval.
  • Platforms may label AI-generated ads, and laws may require disclosure in some countries. You remain responsible for how and where you publish your ads.

9. What we won't create

We don't create fake reviews or testimonials, ads that imitate real people without consent, content using other companies' trademarks or characters without permission, or claims without proof.

10. No guaranteed results

We don't guarantee sales, clicks, return on ad spend or ad approval. Results depend on many things we don't control, including your product, offer, website, budget and the ad platforms.

11. Ending monthly work

Either side can end monthly work with 30 days' written notice. Work already paid for during the notice period will still be delivered.

12. Liability

As far as the law allows, our total liability for a project is limited to the amount you paid for that project. Nothing here removes rights that can't legally be removed.

13. Privacy note

When you start a pilot, we receive your work email, your product link and any notes you add. We use these details only to reply and to manage your project.

  • Your form answers pass through our website and email providers. Payments are handled by our payment provider, and we never see your full card number.
  • We don't sell your details or add you to a newsletter.
  • We keep project records only as long as needed for the work and our accounts.
  • To see, correct or delete your details, email us and we'll reply in writing.
