The problem

Why good brands run out of ads

04

  • 01

    Video takes time

    A static ad is quick. A video needs a script, footage and an editor, so it keeps getting pushed back.

  • 02

    Good ads wear out

    The ad that works today can slow down next month. You need the next idea ready before that happens.

  • 03

    Freelancers need managing

    Briefs, revisions and follow-ups pull you away from your products. A fixed package keeps it simple.

  • 04

    AI tools need direction

    Anyone can buy an AI tool. Someone still has to find the angle, write the hook and check what comes out.
