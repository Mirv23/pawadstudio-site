The problem

Why good brands run out of ads

04

  • 01

    Video takes time

    A static ad is quick. A video needs a script, footage and an editor, so it keeps getting pushed back.

  • 02

    Good ads wear out

    The ad that works today can slow down next month. You need the next idea ready before that happens.

  • 03

    Freelancers need managing

    Briefs, revisions and follow-ups pull you away from your products. A fixed package keeps it simple.

  • 04

    AI tools need direction

    Anyone can buy an AI tool. Someone still has to find the angle, write the hook and check what comes out.

How every ad is made

  1. 01Research first. We study your product, your reviews and the ads running in your category before we write a script.
  2. 02Real references. Your product photos and licensed real-world images guide every shot.
  3. 03AI production. We generate the scenes and keep only the takes that look right.
  4. 04Edit and check. We cut and caption each ad, then check it against a written list before you see it.

Rules we don't break

  1. 01No fake customers. We never invent reviews, testimonials or real-looking buyers.
  2. 02Your product as it is. We never make it look better than what arrives.
  3. 03No unproven claims. A claim only appears if you can back it up.
  4. 04Open about AI. We tell you how it's used. If you don't want it, we'll say whether we can still help.
